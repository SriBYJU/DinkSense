# Google Maps Court Discovery

DinkSense court discovery no longer requires manually logging a court.

- Enter a city, ZIP, or neighborhood, or allow browser geolocation.
- Filter for all, indoor, outdoor, public, or open-play searches.
- The app builds an official Google Maps Search URL with `api=1`.
- The embedded map gives a quick preview; the **Open full Google Maps** button is the authoritative place for current ratings, hours, photos, traffic, and directions.
- Saving a court inside DinkSense is optional and is only for private notes, surface preferences, and crowd memory.

Google Maps is an online service. Match, health, video, and training records are not included in the search query.
